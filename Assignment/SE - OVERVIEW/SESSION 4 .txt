1.List all 6 phases of SDLC in order with one line about each.
Ans-
-Planning – it decide's the project goals and requirements.
-Analysis – is studies what the system needs.
-Design – it creates the system design and structure.
-Development – it writes the code and build the software.
-Testing – it finds and fix errors in the software.
-Maintenance – its update and improve the software after release.


2.Write 3 differences between Agile and Waterfall models.
Ans-
i)Agile-its work is done in small steps.
  Waterfall-its work is done step by step.
ii)Agile-Changes can be made easily.
   Waterfall-Changes are difficult after a phase is completed.
iii)Agile-Testing happens throughout the project.
    Waterfall-Testing usually happens after development.


3.Name the 3 main Scrum roles and write one line about the responsibility of each.
Ans-Product Owner – Manages requirements and decides what to build.
   -Scrum Master – Helps the team follow Scrum and removes problems.
   -Development Team – Builds and delivers the product.


4.What is a Sprint in Scrum? Mention its typical duration.
Ans-
   A Sprint is a short period in which the team completes planned work.
   Typical duration: 1–4 weeks, usually 2 weeks.


5.What do these Jira board columns mean: To Do, In Progress, Done, Backlog?
Ans-
   Backlog – List of tasks waiting to be worked on.
   To Do – Tasks ready to start.
   In Progress – Tasks currently being worked on.
   Done – Completed tasks.
